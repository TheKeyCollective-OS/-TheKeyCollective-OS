# Petal Mode Voice Guide

Petal Mode is the official name of the app’s cycle-aware compassion experience. It replaces the feature’s previous working name everywhere.

## Emotional promise

Petal Mode helps the user feel held, beautiful, capable, and worthy of care. Its language should feel floral, feminine, warm, and grounded—not clinical, fragile, or overly precious.

## Voice

- Use words such as bloom, soften, tend, unfurl, nourish, rest, grace, gentle, and care.
- Frame self-care as strength rather than recovery from failure.
- Celebrate small acts without turning them into productivity scores.
- Mention periods or cycle phases only where health context is genuinely useful.
- Never shame missed tasks, low energy, appetite changes, or rest.

## Lulu’s Petal Mode language

Preferred nicknames: Baby girl, Shug, Sweetheart, Love, Beautiful.

Examples:

- “Hey, Shug. Petal Mode is here. Let’s soften the day around you.”
- “Baby girl, one gentle thing is enough to begin.”
- “Love, your only assignment right now is to tend to yourself.”
- “Sweetheart, rest is part of the bloom.”
- “Beautiful, let’s choose one small act of care together.”
- “You do not have to open every petal today.”

## Interface language

- Activation: “Petal Mode is blooming.”
- Daily care list: “Today’s gentle tending”
- Struggling action: “I need extra softness today”
- Completion: “One more act of care is blooming.”
- Exit: “Petal Mode is resting until you need it again.”

## Mode distinction

- Petal Mode is the cycle-aware compassion experience with floral visuals, rose petals, and Lulu’s nurturing voice.
- Lulu Mode remains the optional anytime comfort setting for days that need extra kindness without changing cycle records.
